- **No other brands:** check shoes, clothes, kit and the corners of the picture (some tools stamp their
  own watermark). A clip carrying another company's mark can't go into the app.
- **77 clips is a lot** — expect it to take several sittings.
- The performer is described once, in the prompt's "Performer" paragraph. Change that paragraph before
  pasting if you'd like someone different.

# Inte-Run demonstration videos — the brief

You're making short demonstration videos for **Inte-Run** ("The Intelligent Training Companion"), my
running-coach app. Each clip shows one warm-up drill, stretch or strength exercise, done with correct
technique, so a runner can copy it. The clips will sit inside the app, so together they must look like
one matching set: the same performer, room, camera and light in every clip, with Inte-Run's branding and
nobody else's. There are 77 clips in all.

## The rules that matter most

1. **Real video only.** Every clip must be genuine video with real, continuous motion. Never still images
   stitched together, never stop-motion, never a slideshow, never a still picture with a zoom or pan, and
   never frames drawn or assembled with code. If you can't make genuine video here, tell me in one line,
   make the two reference images (step 1), and stop. Don't fake it.
2. **Correct technique comes first.** A wrong demonstration can injure a runner. Follow each clip's
   **Show** and **App cue** lines exactly, and keep out everything in its **Avoid** line. If you can't
   show a movement correctly, tell me rather than guess. I'll check every clip before it goes into the
   app.
3. **One look.** Make the reference images first and build every clip from them, so the performer, room,
   camera and light never change.
4. **Inte-Run branding only.** No other brand's logo, name, stripes or marks anywhere, and no text in the
   video.
5. **Be straight with me.** If something here isn't possible — real video, the square shape, the length,
   no sound, the logo — say so plainly rather than working around it quietly.

**Movement.**
- Real-time speed, smooth and controlled, like a good coach demonstrating.
- Strength reps: about 2 seconds down, a brief pause, about 1 second up. Two or three reps unless the

- Holds: completely still apart from calm breathing.

- Drills and jumps: real speed, light and springy.

## The technical spec — so the clips drop straight into the app

The app shows each demonstration in a square white frame, scaled to fit (never cropped), playing on a
loop with the sound off, in both light and dark mode. That's why the set is light.

| | |
|---|---|
| File | MP4 (H.264 video), one file per clip |
| Shape | Square 1:1, 1080 × 1080. If your tool can't make square, use 1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920 and keep the performer and all kit inside the centre square of the frame, so it can be cropped to square without cutting anything off |
| Length | 6 to 10 seconds |
| Loop | One continuous take that starts and ends in the same position, so it loops without a jump (travelling clips: the same point in the stride). No cuts, fades or titles |
| Speed | Real time. No slow motion and no speed ramps |
| Frame rate | Steady, 24 to 30 frames a second |
| Sound | None — no music, voice or sound effects. If your tool always adds sound, tell me and I'll remove it |
| On screen | No text, captions, counters, arrows or graphics. No watermark you can avoid; tell me if your tool adds one |

### The test clip — make this first (folder `strength`)

**1 · `strength/goblet-squat.mp4` — Goblet / bodyweight squat**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: one dumbbell.
- Show: Feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out a little, the dumbbell held upright against the chest with both hands cupping its top end. ### Warm-up drills — folder `warm-up`

Done before a run. The app lists them under "Mobilise" with this line: "Move through a comfortable range —
this is rehearsal and a check on how you feel today, not stretching. Never force it, and never bounce at
the end of the range." Show them relaxed and rhythmic, not as a workout. The app gives each drill as a
name and an amount, shown here as **App shows**.

**2 · `warm-up/ankle-rocks.mp4` — Ankle rocks**
- App shows: "Ankle rocks, 8–12 each side"
- Camera: side-on. Kit: wall panel.
- Show: Standing in a short split stance facing the wall panel, right foot forward, fingertips resting on the panel for balance. Keeping the front heel flat on the floor, rock the front knee gently forward over the toes as far as is comfortable, then back. Smooth and rhythmic, about one rock a second, six to eight rocks.
- Avoid: the front heel lifting; bouncing at the end of the range; the whole body lunging instead of the knee moving.

**3 · `warm-up/leg-swings-front-to-back.mp4` — Leg swings front-to-back**
- App shows: "Leg swings front-to-back, 8–12 each side"
- Camera: side-on. Kit: wall panel.
- Show: Standing tall with the wall panel on the far side from the camera, left hand resting on it, standing knee soft. Swing the right leg forward and back from the hip like a pendulum, starting small and growing to a comfortable range. The torso stays tall and still. Six to eight swings.
- Avoid: leaning or twisting the torso; arching the lower back on the back swing; kicking or flinging the leg at the end of the swing.

**4 · `warm-up/leg-swings-side-to-side.mp4` — Leg swings side-to-side**
- App shows: "Leg swings side-to-side, 8–12 each side"
- Camera: front-on. Kit: wall panel.
- Show: Facing the camera with the wall panel beside the left hip, left hand resting on it, standing knee soft. Swing the right leg across in front of the standing leg and then out to the side, like a pendulum, starting small and growing to a comfortable range. The hips and shoulders stay square to the camera. Six to eight swings.
- Avoid: twisting the hips or torso to make the swing bigger; leaning; flinging the leg.

**5 · `warm-up/walking-lunge-with-a-reach.mp4` — Walking lunge with a reach**
- App shows: "Walking lunge with a reach, 5–8 each side"
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: Moving left to right, take a long step into a lunge and lower until the back knee is just above the floor, front knee over the front foot, torso tall. At the bottom, reach both arms straight up overhead. Lower the arms while stepping the back leg through into the next lunge. Alternate legs, three or four lunges.
- Avoid: the back knee banging the floor; leaning forward; the front knee falling inward; short, choppy steps.

**6 · `warm-up/a-march.mp4` — A-march**
- App shows: "Marching on the spot or A-march, 2 × 15–20 m"
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: Marching slowly forward, tall. Drive one knee up to hip height with the toes pulled up toward the shin while the opposite arm swings forward, elbow bent at about 90°; the standing leg stays tall, up on the ball of the foot. Step the foot down under the hips and switch. A steady rhythm of about two steps a second.
- Avoid: leaning back; the knee stopping below hip height; the foot landing out in front of the body; floppy toes.

**7 · `warm-up/a-skip.mp4` — A-skip**
- App shows: "A-skip, 2 × 15–20 m"
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: The A-march with a skip: a small, light hop on the standing leg each time the other knee drives up to hip height, toes pulled up. That foot then strikes down actively under the hips while the opposite arm drives in a running action. Light, rhythmic and tall, travelling slowly forward.
- Avoid: big bounding hops; leaning back; landing on the heel or out in front of the body.

**8 · `warm-up/strides.mp4` — Strides**
- App says: "A stride is a smooth build, not a sprint."
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: From an easy jog, build speed smoothly over about four seconds to fast, relaxed running — quick, but well short of a sprint — hold it for a couple of seconds, then ease back down to a jog. Tall posture, quick light steps landing under the body, relaxed shoulders, hands and face, arms swinging forward and back.
- Avoid: an all-out sprint; a strained face or clenched fists; reaching out with long strides; leaning back.

**9 · `warm-up/high-knees.mp4` — High knees**
- App shows: "High knees, 2 × 10–20 m"
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: Quick, light running steps travelling slowly forward, each knee driving up to about hip height, each foot landing on its ball under the hips, posture tall, arms pumping in a running action.
- Avoid: leaning back; landing on the heels; the knees staying well below hip height; stamping.

**10 · `warm-up/heel-recovery-ankling.mp4` — Heel recovery / ankling**
- App shows: "Heel recovery / ankling, 2 × 15–20 m"
- Camera: side-on, tracking. Kit: none.
- Show: Short, quick steps travelling slowly forward, posture tall, arms in a relaxed running action. Each foot lifts only a little: the heel comes up under the hips to about the height of the other ankle, then the foot comes back down onto its ball, under the body. Quick and light, with springy ankles.
- Avoid: big kicks of the heel up toward the bottom; long strides; landing on the heels; bending forward at the waist.

### Stretches after a run — folder `stretches`

The app offers these after a run: "Optional, and it is fine to skip. A few minutes of easing out while you
are still warm — it is about range of movement and coming down from the run, nothing more than that."
Each clip takes about 8 seconds: ease into the stretch over about 2 seconds, hold it completely still with
slow, calm breathing for about 4 seconds, then ease back out to the start position over about 2 seconds,
so it loops. One-sided stretches show the right side.

**11 · `stretches/standing-quad-stretch.mp4` — Standing quad stretch**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: wall panel.
- Show: Standing tall facing the wall panel, left hand on it. Bend the right knee, take the right ankle in the right hand behind, and ease the heel toward the bottom — knees side by side, hips square, pelvis slightly tucked, torso tall. Hold, then ease out.
- App cue: "Hold a wall, take your ankle behind you and keep your knees together and your hips square."
- Avoid: the bent knee drifting forward or out to the side; arching the lower back; leaning forward; yanking the foot.

**12 · `stretches/standing-hamstring-stretch.mp4` — Standing hamstring stretch**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: low step.
- Show: The right heel on the low step in front, toes pointing up, the right leg straight but not locked, the standing knee soft, hands on the hips. Hinge forward from the hips with a long, flat back until a stretch is felt at the back of the thigh. Hold, then come back up.
- App cue: "Heel on a low step, leg straight, hinge forward from the hips with a flat back."
- Avoid: rounding the back; reaching down for the toes; bending the front knee a lot; bouncing.

**13 · `stretches/calf-stretch-against-a-wall.mp4` — Calf stretch against a wall**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: wall panel.
- Show: Facing the wall panel with both hands on it at shoulder height. The right leg is back — straight, heel pressed down, toes pointing forward — and the left knee is bent in front. Ease the hips toward the panel and hold. Then bend the back knee slightly, keeping that heel down, and hold again. Ease out. This one runs about 10 seconds.
- App cue: "Back leg straight, heel pressed down, hips forward. Then bend that knee slightly to reach lower down."
- Avoid: the back heel lifting; the back foot turning out; arching the lower back.

**14 · `stretches/kneeling-hip-flexor-stretch.mp4` — Kneeling hip flexor stretch**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: mat.
- Show: Half-kneeling on the mat: right knee down, left foot flat in front with the left knee over the ankle, hands on the hips, torso tall. Tuck the tailbone under, flattening the lower back, and ease the hips a little forward while staying tall. Hold, then ease back.
- App cue: "Half-kneeling, tuck your tailbone under and ease your hips forward. Stay tall rather than leaning."
- Avoid: leaning the torso forward; arching the lower back; lunging far forward; the front knee falling inward.

**15 · `stretches/figure-four-glute-stretch.mp4` — Figure-four glute stretch**
- Camera: three-quarter from beyond the feet, on the performer's right, about 60 cm off the floor — so the crossed shin, the other thigh and the hands are all in view (from exactly side-on the crossed knee points into the lens and hides them). Kit: mat.
- Show: Lying on the back on the mat, head toward the left of the frame, knees bent, feet flat. Cross the right ankle over the left thigh just above the knee, thread the hands behind the left thigh, and gently draw that thigh toward the chest. Head and shoulders stay relaxed on the mat. Hold, then lower back to the start.
- App cue: "On your back, ankle across the opposite thigh, and draw that thigh towards your chest."
- Avoid: pulling on the knee or shin; lifting the head and shoulders; twisting the hips.

**16 · `stretches/childs-pose.mp4` — Child's pose**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: mat.
- Show: From hands and knees on the mat, sit the hips back toward the heels with the arms long in front, palms on the mat, forehead resting toward the mat, back long and relaxed. Hold with slow, visible breathing, then come back up to hands and knees.
- App cue: "Sit back onto your heels, arms long in front, and let your breathing slow down."
- Avoid: forcing the hips down; shrugging the shoulders up to the ears.

### Strength — folder `strength`

Grouped as the app groups them. Light-to-moderate weights throughout.

#### Squat

**17 · `strength/step-up.mp4` — Step-up**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: low step.
- Show: Right foot flat on the low step. Drive through that foot to stand tall on the step, bringing the left foot up beside it, then lower the left foot back to the floor slowly, under control. The right foot stays on the step for all three reps.
- App cue: "Drive through the top foot to stand tall, then lower with control. Start with a low step."
- Avoid: pushing off with the bottom foot; the knee falling inward; leaning far forward; dropping down.

**18 · `strength/box-squat.mp4` — Box squat**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: box, just behind the performer.
- Show: Feet shoulder-width, arms reaching forward for balance. Sit back onto the box under control, pause for a moment while staying braced — no slumping or rocking — then drive back up to standing.
- App cue: "Sit back onto the box under control, pause briefly without relaxing, then drive back up."
- Avoid: dropping onto the box; rocking back to get up; relaxing on the box.

**19 · `strength/front-rack-squat.mp4` — Front-rack squat**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: barbell, light load.
- Show: The barbell resting across the front of the shoulders, fingertips under the bar, elbows high and pointing forward. Sit straight down, the hips dropping between the heels, with an upright torso until the thighs are about level with the floor, then stand.
- App cue: "Weight held at the shoulders keeps you upright — sit down between your hips rather than back."
- Avoid: the elbows dropping; the torso tipping forward; heels lifting.

**20 · `strength/step-down.mp4` — Step-down**
- Camera: three-quarter, so the standing knee and both hips are in view. Kit: low step.
- Show: Standing tall on the low step on the right leg, the left foot (the free, "trailing" foot) hovering just off the front edge. Slowly bend the right knee and hip to lower the left heel until it lightly touches the floor in front — about three seconds down — then straighten the right leg to rise again. No weight goes onto the left foot. Two reps.
- App cue: "Lower the trailing foot to the floor slowly, controlling the knee — the lowering is the exercise, not the step itself."
- Avoid: dropping down; pushing off the floor with the lowering foot; the standing knee falling inward; the hips tilting.

**21 · `strength/pistol-squat.mp4` — Pistol squat**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: box, just behind the performer as a target.
- Show: Standing on the right leg, the left leg held straight out in front just off the floor, arms reaching forward. Sit down slowly on the right leg as far as control allows, lightly touching the box, then stand back up on the same leg. Two reps.
- App cue: "One leg extended in front, sit the standing leg down as far as control allows. Use a box under the hips at first."
- Avoid: the standing knee falling inward; the heel lifting; dropping onto the box; using momentum.

#### Hinge

**22 · `strength/romanian-deadlift.mp4` — Romanian deadlift**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: pair of dumbbells.
- Show: Standing tall with a dumbbell in each hand in front of the thighs, feet hip-width, knees soft. Push the hips back with a flat back, the dumbbells sliding close down the front of the legs to about mid-shin, until the hamstrings are stretched; then drive the hips forward to stand tall.
- App cue: "Soft knees, push your hips back with a flat back until you feel the hamstrings, then stand tall."
- Avoid: rounding the back; bending the knees into a squat; the weights drifting away from the legs; leaning back at the top.

**23 · `strength/single-leg-rdl.mp4` — Single-leg RDL**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: none.
- Show: Standing on the right leg with a soft knee, arms hanging. Hinge forward from the hip as the left leg reaches straight back for balance — back flat, hips level — until the torso and back leg are close to level with the floor, or as far as control allows. Return slowly. Two slow reps.
- App cue: "Hinge forward on one leg as the other reaches back for balance. A slower tempo matters more than the range."
- Avoid: the hips twisting open; rounding the back; the standing knee locking or bending a lot; rushing.

**24 · `strength/kettlebell-deadlift.mp4` — Kettlebell deadlift**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: one kettlebell.
- Show: Feet hip-width with the kettlebell on the floor between them, level with the middle of the feet. Hinge — hips back, knees bending as much as needed to reach the handle with a flat back — and grip it with both hands, shoulders over the bell. Brace and stand tall by driving the floor away, then hinge to set it back down.
- App cue: "Weight between your feet, hinge to grip it with a flat back, then stand by driving the floor away."
- Avoid: rounding the back; lifting with the arms; squatting it up with the hips low; leaning back at the top.

**25 · `strength/barbell-deadlift.mp4` — Barbell deadlift**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: barbell, moderate load.
- Show: The bar, on full-size plates, on the floor over the middle of the feet. Bend the knees until the shins touch the bar — hips above the knees, shoulders over the bar, back flat, chest up — and grip it just outside the knees. Brace, then stand by pushing the floor away, the bar staying close to the legs all the way; lower it the same way under control. Two reps.
- App cue: "Bar close to the shins, brace before you pull, and stand by pushing the floor away rather than yanking the bar."
- Avoid: rounding the back; jerking the bar; the bar drifting forward; leaning back at the top.

**26 · `strength/good-morning.mp4` — Good morning**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: none — hands behind the head.
- Show: Standing tall, feet hip-width, knees soft, fingertips lightly behind the head with the elbows wide. Hinge the hips back with a flat back until the torso is roughly 45° forward — a small range — then stand tall. Slow.
- App cue: "Hips hinge back while the bar (or your hands, behind your head) stays over them. Small range, strict form."
- Avoid: rounding the back; pulling on the neck; going too low; bending the knees into a squat.

**27 · `strength/kettlebell-swing.mp4` — Kettlebell swing**
- Camera: side-on. Kit: one kettlebell.
- Show: Feet a little wider than the hips. ## Appendix B — what wiring the clips in will involve

Notes for a later session; none of this is done yet.

- **How demos play today.** Strength demos are animated WebP images inlined as data URIs (`EX_ANIM[slug]`,
  stills in `EX_STILL`), drawn by `exVisual()` and played full size by `openExDemo()` — on the session
  sheet and on Support › Strength & mobility (`strengthView()` → `libCard()`). Stretches play four PNG
  frames (`ASM_FRAMES`, `asmSet()`), mirrored for the right side. Warm-up drills have no demo at all: they
  are one line of text in `warmupHtml()` and in the live "Mobilise" step (`withGeneratedWarmup()`).
- **The frame.** Square, white in both themes, `object-fit: contain` — `.ex-webp`, `.exlb-in img
  { aspect-ratio: 1 }`, `.asm-frame` — with about a 9% safe margin in the old pack (46 px of 512). The
  brief asks for exactly that shape.
- **An MP4 needs a `<video muted loop playsinline autoplay>`** with a `poster` still; the `<img>` that
  plays the WebPs cannot play it. Keep honouring Reduce Motion the way `asmReduceMotion()` does: show
  the still and don't autoplay.
- **Size and delivery are a decision for the owner first.** The page inlines every asset and is already
  about 14 MB, so 77 clips cannot be inlined. Serving them as files, as `docs/voices/` does, is a second
  exception to "no external network assets", and the native app only picks up new files on an Xcode
  rebuild (the over-the-air update carries `index.html` alone).
- **Re-encode before shipping:** strip the audio (`-an`), H.264 with `yuv420p` and `+faststart`, crop
  to square if a clip came back 16:9 or 9:16, scale to about 720 × 720, CRF around 28. ffmpeg is not
  installed on this Mac (`brew install ffmpeg`).
- **Mirroring.** The stretch player mirrors the art for the right side. A mirrored clip shows a chest logo
  backwards, so either don't mirror (say "now the other side") or mirror only clips with a plain top.
- **Two warm-up drills never reach a runner.** `pickMovements()` adds `ankling` for hard and maximal
  sessions and then cuts the list to five, which drops it every time; `high_knees` is never picked at
  all. A sweep of 184 real sessions × 4 abilities × 7 conditions gave both 0. Clips 9 and 10 are briefed
  anyway so either can be switched on later; whether to change the engine is the owner's call.
- **The owner checks every clip's technique before it ships.** A wrong demonstration can injure a runner.
- `test/videos-brief.test.ts` holds this file to the code: an exercise, stretch or warm-up drill with no
  clip here fails it, and so does a cue quoted here that no longer matches the app's words.
