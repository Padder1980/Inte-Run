Cross-cutting principles

Three things the brief demands everywhere — not a single domain, but a property every recommendation should carry.

Show uncertainty

Confidence ranges, not false precision — "estimated VO₂max 52–56", never "54.3". Label evidence strong / uncertain / absent.

Tag provenance

Every recommendation carries its evidence: population studied, sex, training level, age, event, evidence quality.

Refer, never diagnose

Educate, screen, and route to a qualified professional. The app must never claim to be a doctor, physio or dietitian.

The central product principle

The brief's own summary of what a credible companion does. The engine already lives the first steps; the last two are the weakest today.
