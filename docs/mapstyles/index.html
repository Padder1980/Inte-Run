Mapbox

Worth a look: its Outdoors style draws trails, paths and contour lines, which the CARTO maps above do not. That is a real difference for running, not just a nicer palette.

Mapbox needs an access token. ⚠️ Paste yours below — it is kept in this browser only and is never committed. A public pk. token is designed to sit in client-side code, unlike a Google key, but it is still yours: restrict it by URL in your Mapbox account, and know that the native app's interun://app origin cannot be URL-restricted.

The six above are OpenStreetMap data rendered by CARTO: no key, no account, no per-view cost — which is why they are the default options. Mapbox needs a token and an account but its Outdoors style shows trails and contours, which none of the others do. Google Maps is absent for a different reason: its key cannot be kept private in a public page, it bills per view, and its terms forbid the canvas export the share card depends on.
