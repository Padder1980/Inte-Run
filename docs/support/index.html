Support

Help with Inte-Run, the running coach app.

Email

Inte-Run is made by one person, Adam Palmer, in England. For help, questions about your information, or to report a problem, email adam.palmer86@gmail.com.

It helps to include what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot. The lines under Profile › Your data › This version say which version of the app you’re using.

Not for emergencies. Inte-Run can’t give medical advice by email. In an emergency, call 999 (or your local emergency number). For pain or an injury, see a GP or a physiotherapist.

Things you can do in the app

  • See what goes online, and switch things off: Profile › Your data.
  • Back up, or move to a new phone: Profile › Your data › Export a backup, then Restore on the new phone.
  • Disconnect Strava: Profile › Apps & devices › Strava.
  • Delete everything: Profile › Your data › Delete everything.
  • Check a symptom or an injury: Support › Injury & symptoms. It’s general guidance, not a diagnosis.
