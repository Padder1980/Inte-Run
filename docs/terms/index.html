1. About these terms

Inte-Run is a running coach app made by Adam Palmer, a one-person developer in England. In these terms, “we” and “us” mean him, and “you” means the person using the app.

These terms cover the Inte-Run iPhone app, its Apple Watch app and the web version at padder1980.github.io/Inte-Run. By using Inte-Run, you agree to them. What happens to your information is in the privacy policy.

2. Who Inte-Run is for

Inte-Run is designed for runners aged 12 and up. For runners aged 12 to 17 it has its own programme, which keeps within UK Athletics’ limits for each age.

If you’re under 18: please read these terms, or the simple version, with a parent, carer or another adult you trust, and make sure they’re happy for you to use Inte-Run, especially before you start a plan or run on your own.

If you’re a parent or carer, you decide whether Inte-Run is right for your child, and you’re responsible for supervising their training. The app has no parental controls. For anyone under 18 its privacy settings start switched off, and some features aren’t available under 13 (see the privacy policy).

3. A coach, not a doctor

This is general guidance, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess you properly.

Everything Inte-Run gives you, including plans, paces, spoken coaching, Ask Alfie, the injury and fuelling guides and the check-ins, is general information about training. It isn’t medical advice, and the app can’t examine you.

Talk to a doctor before you start if you have a heart, lung or other medical condition, are pregnant or have recently had a baby, are getting over an injury or illness, have ever fainted or had chest pain while exercising, or aren’t sure whether it’s safe for you to run.

Stop and get help. Chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, a sudden severe headache, or an injury you can’t walk on: stop, and get medical help straight away. In an emergency, call 999 (or your local emergency number). Don’t use an app to decide.

Pain that gets worse as you run, changes how you move or wakes you at night deserves a GP’s or a physiotherapist’s opinion. And if something feels wrong while the app says it’s fine, believe yourself.

4. Running safely

You’re responsible for your own safety when you run (and a parent or carer is, for a young runner). Take care near traffic, tell someone where you’re going, dress for the heat, the cold and the dark, and don’t look at your phone or watch when it isn’t safe to. The heat advice uses a weather forecast, and forecasts can be wrong.

5. Your plan is yours

Inte-Run suggests; you decide. It never changes a pace, plan or target on its own: every change it proposes is your choice, and you can say no.

Distances, paces and heart rates come from your phone’s GPS and sensors and from your watch. They can be wrong, for example under trees, between tall buildings or in tunnels.

6. Ask Alfie

Alfie is an AI coach. It can be wrong, out of date, or sure of something that isn’t true, so don’t rely on it for anything to do with your health or safety. When its online answers are on, please don’t type other people’s personal details. Online answers are limited each hour and each day, and may sometimes be unavailable; Alfie then answers on your phone instead.

7. Your content and sharing

What you put into Inte-Run, including your runs, notes, photos, videos, posts, stories and journals, is yours. It stays on your phone unless you share it.

  • When you share a card or a photo, you choose where it goes, and you’re responsible for it. Think about what it shows: a route can show where you live.
  • Only share photos or videos of other people if they’re happy for you to (and, for a child, their parent or carer too).
  • Never share anything unlawful, hateful or cruel.

8. Other services

Strava, Apple Health, the Apple Watch, the map and weather services, and calendar apps are run by other companies under their own terms. We’re not responsible for them, and a feature that relies on one may stop working if they change. Strava is for runners aged 13 and over, which is Strava’s rule.

9. Using Inte-Run fairly

Please don’t misuse the app or its server. That means not trying to get around the limits on Ask Alfie or Strava, not overloading, attacking or taking apart the server, and not using Inte-Run to break the law or to harm anyone. We may block a device that does.
