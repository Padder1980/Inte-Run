First, set things up

👤 Your turn
Create the app in Apple's system
About 10 minutes
  • On your Mac or phone, go to appstoreconnect.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple Developer account (the one you paid the £99 for)
  • Click My Apps at the top
  • Click the + button in the top-left corner → choose New App
  • Fill in these fields exactly:
    • Platforms: tick iOS
    • Name: Inte-Run
    • Primary language: English (UK)
    • Bundle ID: pick com.interun.app from the list
    • SKU: type interun-ios
    • User Access: Full Access
  • Click Create
Bundle ID not in the list? Click the small link that says "Register a new Bundle ID" and type com.interun.app exactly. Then come back and choose it.
✋ Don't fill anything else in
You don't need App Store screenshots, descriptions, or pricing yet. Leave it all blank. The only job here is creating the record.

Then, wait for Apple
3
⏳ Just wait
Apple checks the build automatically
30–60 minutes — go make a cup of tea
  • In App Store Connect, click Inte-Run → then TestFlight at the top
  • You'll see your build with a yellow Processing dot. This is normal — Apple is scanning it automatically.
  • When the dot turns green, Apple emails you. The email asks about encryption. Answer: "No, my app doesn't use encryption" (HTTPS is exempted, you don't need to worry about it).
  • The build should now say "Ready to Test" — not "Missing Compliance"
Still saying "Processing" after an hour? Check your email — Apple usually sends a message if something went wrong. The subject line will say what the problem is.
Now, install it yourself first
4
👤 Your turn
Set yourself up as a tester and install the app
About 10 minutes
  • In App Store Connect on your Mac:
  • Go to Inte-Run → TestFlight → Internal Testing
  • Click Create New Group → name it Owner + close friends → tick Enable automatic distribution → click Create
  • On the group page: click Add Testers → tick your own name
  • In the Builds section: click + and add the build you just uploaded
  • On your iPhone:
  • Install the TestFlight app from the App Store if you don't have it already
  • Wait for the email from Apple that says "You have been invited to test Inte-Run"
  • Tap View in TestFlight in the email → tap Install
  • Open the app → go to Support → Your data → This version → confirm the build number matches what Claude told you
⚠️ If you still have the Xcode version on your phone
Delete it first. Long press → Remove App → Delete. Your data is safe — it will be there when you reinstall via TestFlight (same bundle ID).

If something goes wrong

Common problems and what to do

My build isn't showing up in App Store Connect
Wait 15 more minutes. If still nothing, check your email — Apple sends a message if the upload failed, and it names exactly what went wrong.
A tester says the invitation email never arrived
Double-check the email address you typed. Ask them to search their spam for emails from apple.com. You can resend invitations from the TestFlight page.
It says the app has expired
TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. Just ask Claude to build again (step 7 above) and everyone gets the new version automatically.
The coach isn't speaking or the map isn't showing
Usually permissions weren't granted. Tell the tester to open iPhone Settings → scroll to Inte-Run → make sure Location says "While Using" and that Notifications are on.
Something is broken and testers are complaining
In App Store Connect → TestFlight, find the broken build and click Expire. Nobody new will get it. Then ask Claude to fix and build again.
