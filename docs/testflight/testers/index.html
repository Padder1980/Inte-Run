Thank you for testing Inte-Run

For the people trying early versions through Apple’s TestFlight. Updated 28 September 2026

The short version
  • Open Apple’s invitation email on your iPhone, install TestFlight, and tap Install.
  • Use it for real for a week or two: set up a plan, and go for a run.
  • When something is wrong, or just confusing, send Adam a screenshot and a sentence.
  • It’s an early version. Some things aren’t finished, and things will change.

1. Getting the app

  1. You’ll get an email from Apple saying you’ve been invited to test Inte-Run. Open it on your iPhone.
  2. If you haven’t got it, install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  3. In the email, tap View in TestFlight, then Install. Then open Inte-Run.

Apple Watch: the watch app comes with it. On your iPhone, open the Watch app, scroll to Available Apps, find Inte-Run and tap Install.

New versions arrive through TestFlight, which tells you when there’s one. Each version works for 90 days. If Adam once put Inte-Run on your phone from his Mac, don’t delete it: deleting an app deletes your runs with it. Save a backup first (Profile › Your data › Export a backup), then install from TestFlight over the top, which keeps your data.

2. What to try

  • Set up a plan. The first question is about your health. Please answer it honestly.
  • Record a short run outside. Check that the map draws, the coach speaks, and the run appears in your Logbook afterwards.
  • Look at your week. Try moving a session to another day.
  • Ask Alfie something about your plan.
  • Try a strength session from your plan.
  • Make a share card from a run.
  • If you have an Apple Watch, start a run from the watch.

3. What isn’t ready yet

  • Garmin isn’t in the app yet. The application to Garmin is in, and it’s waiting for their approval.
  • iPhone only. There’s no Android version.
  • No accounts. Your runs and plan stay on your phone. To move to a new phone, use Profile › Your data › Export a backup, then Restore on the new one.
  • Strava works if you’re 13 or over, which is Strava’s own rule.
  • Ask Alfie’s online answers are optional and limited each day. When they’re off or used up, Alfie answers on your phone instead.

4. How to report a problem

The quickest way: take a screenshot in Inte-Run. TestFlight offers to Share Beta Feedback: add a sentence and send it, and it goes straight to Adam.

Or email adam.palmer86@gmail.com, or text Adam.

Whichever you choose, please say:

  • what you did,
  • what you expected to happen,
  • and what happened instead.

It helps a lot to include the lines under Profile › Your data › This version: they say exactly which version you have. A screenshot of them is perfect.

If the app crashes, TestFlight sends Adam a crash report by itself, but please still say what you were doing. A wrong colour or a confusing word is worth sending too.

5. Your privacy while testing

Inte-Run keeps your runs, your plan and your answers on your phone. The privacy policy (and the simple version) says exactly what goes online, and why.

TestFlight is Apple’s service. It lets Adam see your name and email address, which versions you’ve installed, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send.

6. Please

  • Keep it to yourself for now. Please don’t share the app, or screenshots of it, publicly yet.
  • Inte-Run is a coach, not a doctor. If something hurts or you feel unwell, stop and get proper help. In an emergency, call 999.

Thank you. Every note helps.
